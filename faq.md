# Frequently Asked Questions

---

## General

### What is Bitflinger TV?

Bitflinger TV is a smart launcher for Android TV / Google TV, Android, and iOS. It surfaces personalized movie and TV show recommendations pulled from your streaming services, lets you manage a watchlist, and allows you to signal what you like or dislike — so recommendations improve over time.

### What platforms does Bitflinger TV support?

- **Android TV / Google TV** (primary platform, D-pad optimized)
- **Android**
- **iOS**
- **Web** (not launched yet)

---

## Account & Sign-In

### How do I switch accounts?

Tap the profile avatar in the top-left corner of the home screen. A Google TV account picker will appear, showing your existing accounts and an option to add a new one.

### Is my data backed up?

Yes. Your account, watchlist, and preferences are stored on our servers. If you clear app data or reinstall, signing back in will restore everything automatically.

## Content & Recommendations

### How do I tell the app what I like?

On any title's detail screen, tap **Like** (👍) or **Dislike** (👎). These signals are used to improve your recommendations over time. Interacting with a title also serves as an indication that you've seen it — there is no separate "Mark as Watched" action.

### What does "Will You Like This?" mean?

On the media detail screen, this section compares the title to others and gives a high level, "you'll probably like this if" type of recommendation.

### Why are my content rows empty?

This is likely a caching issue. See the [Troubleshooting Guide — Content Issues](troubleshooting.md#2-content-issues) for steps to clear the cache and reload fresh content.

### Can I control which content rows appear on the home screen?

Yes. Open **Settings → Media Lists** (tap the gear icon in the top-right corner). Each row (Popular, Watchlist, Apps, New, Animation) has a visibility toggle.

## Streaming Services

### What streaming services are supported?

Bitflinger TV auto-detects the streaming apps installed on your device. Open **Settings → Media Sources** and toggle **Auto Detect Sources (Android Only)** to let the app find your apps. Streaming sources with pricing (free, subscription, buy/rent) and episode counts are shown on each title's detail screen.

### Can I rank or prioritize streaming services?

Not yet — this feature is planned. Currently, sources are auto-detected with no manual ordering. [Vote on this issue](https://github.com/bitflinger-llc/tv_support/issues/2) or open a new one to let us know this matters to you.

## Search

### How do I search for a title?

Tap the magnifying glass icon in the top-right of the home screen. You can type using the on-screen keyboard or hold the microphone button 🎤 on your remote to use voice search — voice is recommended on TV where typing with a D-pad is slow.

---

## Appearance & Settings

### Can I change the look of the app?

Yes. Open **Settings → Theme** (gear icon, top-right). You can:

- Toggle the **Dynamic Background** on or off — when enabled, an animated background shifts colors to match the currently focused content.
- Pick an **accent color** (Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, and more).

### Do my settings carry over if I reinstall the app?

Theme color and content row visibility preferences are restored when you sign back in, as they are tied to your account on our servers.
